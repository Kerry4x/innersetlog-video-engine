**Chosen name (Kerry, 2026-11-16): No New Thing**, from KJV Ecclesiastes 1:9.

The concept: Bible stories (KJV) told story-first, then the same human mechanism today. Thesis: *same human, new package.*
Rule for the name: say what the page is about, never sound like a clay or copycat page.

## 4. Narration voice
**What it has to do:** carry a story with weight but never preach; sound like someone telling you something true, close to the mic.
**Pace:** the clay reference reads at 197–218 words/min; for scripture lines we slow to ~150–170 and keep the modern section near ~190.

**ElevenLabs Voice Design prompts to try** (describe the voice; generate a few, keep the best):
1. *Warm older storyteller:* "Middle-aged to older male narrator, warm and low, calm and unhurried, slight gravel, speaks close to the microphone like he is telling a story by a fire, sincere, never theatrical, neutral accent, clean studio recording."
2. *Grounded younger voice:* "Male narrator in his thirties, clear and grounded, conversational but serious, steady pace, a little intimate, neutral accent, no announcer tone, clean studio recording."
3. *Quiet authority (female):* "Female narrator in her forties, calm, warm, low-pitched, confident and quiet, measured pacing, storytelling tone, neutral accent, clean studio recording."

**Audition line** (use the same text for every candidate so they compare fairly):
> "Around 1000 BC, a prophet went to the most powerful king in Israel with a story about a stolen lamb. By the time the story was over, the king had passed judgment on himself without knowing it. 'Thou art the man.'"

Pick on: does the KJV line sound natural, not costume-like; does the hook pull you in; would you listen for ten minutes.

## 6. Reference: how @clayrified_ sets up its page (screenshot, 2026-11-16)
- **Numbers:** 78 posts, 339K followers (their end card months earlier showed 186K).
- **Avatar:** the page name in big clay letters with one character's face peeking from underneath; reads at thumbnail size.
- **Bio (4 lines):** category "Reel creator" · a one-line promise ("Providing value, clay by clay" + 2 emoji) · what they sell ("Shop • Brands • Course") · one link page.
- **Highlights:** Exclusive, Shop, Community (all business; added once there was something to sell).
- **Pinned:** their three biggest videos (two monks, the chalk line, the golden Buddha).
- **Cover template, the same on every post:** 2–5 words in big chunky clay letters (mostly blue, sometimes red or gold) across the top, one character from the story in the middle. The words are the **hook or a quote from the turn** ("HE'S STILL CARRYING HER", "MAKE THIS LINE SHORTER", "THAT'S HIS MOM", "WE DON'T PAY TAXES"), never the lesson.

## 7. What we take for No New Thing (our own version, not theirs)
- **Avatar:** "No New Thing" lettering in our own material, with one of our characters; must read at small size.
- **Cover template:** one consistent layout on every post: a 2–5 word hook or quote from the story's turn + the key character. Our lettering should not be blue clay; an idea that fits the page is letters **carved in stone like a tablet**, or warm terracotta.
- **Bio:** category + one-line promise + (later) one link. No shop/course line until there is one.
- **Pin** the three best performers once we have them; highlights later.
