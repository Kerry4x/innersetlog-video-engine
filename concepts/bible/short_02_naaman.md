## Accuracy notes (KJV)
- The account is **2 Kings 5:1–16**. Naaman, "captain of the host of the king of Syria… a mighty man in valour, but he was a leper" (5:1).
- The gift: "ten talents of silver, and six thousand pieces of gold, and ten changes of raiment" (5:5). The arrival with horses and chariot, and Elisha sending a messenger instead of coming out (5:9–10).
- His anger and his expectation that Elisha would "strike his hand over the place" and that the rivers of Damascus (Abana and Pharpar) were better (5:11–12).
- The servants: "if the prophet had bid thee do some great thing, wouldest thou not have done it?" (5:13). Healed "like unto the flesh of a little child" (5:14).
- Elisha refuses the gift: "I will receive none" (5:16).
- Left out for length: the captive Israelite servant girl who pointed him to Elisha (5:2–3). Worth including in a long-form version.
