## What the winning format is

- **One small, specific, real thing** (a practice, a trick, a job, an object, an experiment) from history, engineering, psychology or law. Rarely a famous event.
- **Titles promise a hidden mechanism**, never a category: *How X worked*, *Why X*, *What X really meant*, *The [object] that [surprising effect]*. The word "history" never appears.
- The topic pool is "anything recorded that makes you ask *wait, why?*", which is effectively bottomless.
