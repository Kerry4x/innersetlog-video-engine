## 5. What makes it work

1. **You-are-there hook:** second person + a physical sensation ("you feel it") before any fact.
2. **Callbacks:** the opening "rule" and the "knocking" come back at 3:44 and 9:32. Loops close.
3. **Ranked superlatives** give each section a headline.
4. **The "what would you do?" test** turns facts into a game the viewer plays along with.
5. **Jokes as graphics:** every quip in the script gets its own visual gag.
6. **Twist + reversal:** "it's not the monster; we are" adds emotional weight in the last 2 minutes.
