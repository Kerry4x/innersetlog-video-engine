## 5. What makes it work

1. **"What would X ACTUALLY feel like" + second person** = an infinite premise series (any power, any scenario), always about *you*.
2. **Realism through mundane detail** (the alarm time, the coffee, the manager's verbal tic) makes the fantasy believable.
3. **Cold open → "let's see how you got here"** guarantees a payoff the viewer waits for.
4. **Failed tests and limits** (can't fly, nothing from the hands) are funny and credible.
5. **Emotional landing:** loneliness after the win.
6. **Cheap art, rich writing:** limited animation on 2s/3s; the script carries the value.
